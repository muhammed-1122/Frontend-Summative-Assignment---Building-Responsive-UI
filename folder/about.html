About Penniwise

Your Friendly Student Budget Buddy

Our Mission

Penniwise was built to solve a common problem for students: managing money effectively. We believe that financial literacy is a crucial skill, and our mission is to provide a simple, intuitive, and powerful tool to help you track your spending, stay on budget, and achieve your financial goals without the stress.

Key Features

Dashboard At-a-Glance

Visualize your income, expenses, and budget progress with clear charts and summaries.

Multi-Currency Tracking

Log transactions in RWF, USD, or NGN, and see all your finances converted to your chosen base currency.

Custom Categories

Organize your spending your way. Add, remove, and manage categories to fit your lifestyle.

Data Management

Your data is yours. Easily export your records to CSV or back up your entire application state to a JSON file.
